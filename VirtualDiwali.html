Overview

A VR experience that recreates the festive atmosphere of Diwali in a virtual space. Users can play with sparklers, enjoy fireworks, and capture the essence of the Indian Cultural Festival. The project focuses on creating an immersive way to experience traditional festivities without physical limitations, and in a risk-free, virtual setting.

Goals

  • Immersive Diwali experience
  • Safe festive celebrations
  • Cultural appreciation
  • Intuitive interactions

Outcome

I built this project to bring the feel of Diwali into VR, with lights, fireworks, and a safe way to celebrate the festival in a virtual space.
